# Epinnox Brand

Epinnox is the capital-intelligence and market-research layer that serves Benjamin.

## Brand role

- **Epinnox sees and interprets markets.**
- **Benjamin decides how capital should be managed.**
- Epinnox Terminal is the operator-facing environment for research, backtesting, scanning, paper qualification, and eventually governed live execution.

## Primary identity

- Name: **EPINNOX**
- Product surface: **EPINNOX TERMINAL**
- Brand line: **Intelligence Moves Capital**
- Product relationship line: **Capital intelligence · Serving Benjamin**
- Functional language: **Strategy · Intelligence · Execution**

## Visual direction

The mark combines an `E`, orbital motion, and an upward trajectory. The orbit represents continuous observation and feedback; the rising path represents capital movement and decision support.

Primary visual language:

- black command-center surfaces
- white typography and data hierarchy
- restrained emerald signal/accent color
- minimal glow reserved for active/qualified/connected states
- dense but legible trading-workstation information architecture

Avoid turning the interface into decorative neon. Emerald should communicate state, intelligence, qualification, and motion rather than becoming a background color.
